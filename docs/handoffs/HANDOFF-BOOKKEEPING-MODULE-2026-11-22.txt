3. FINANCIAL DATA SOURCES & ANTI-DOUBLE-COUNTING RULES
============================================================
- Invoices (`invoices`):
  - Only `invoiceStatus === 'issued'` contributes to Period Billed Revenue (by `invoiceDate` in Asia/Jakarta) and Customer Outstanding Receivable (`SUM(balanceDue)` across all issued invoices).
  - `void` invoices are excluded from both billed revenue and receivables.
  - `invoice.financialSnapshot.totalAmount` and `invoice.amountPaid` are NEVER added to Cash Received.
- Customer Cash Receipts (`paymentTransactions`):
  - Customer Cash In = `SUM(paymentTransactions where transactionType == 'payment') - SUM(paymentTransactions where transactionType == 'reversal')` filtered by `receivedAt`.
  - Payment reversals remain owned exclusively by Payment & Bill (`/admin/payment-and-bill`).
- Commission & Fee Accruals (`bookkeepingEntries`):
  - Preserves existing 13-field `commission-and-fee` entries without forced backfill migration.
  - Staff Commission and Recording Operator Fee accruals are treated as expense obligations (`expense_accrual`), NOT cash out, until paid via settlements.
  - `commissionBatches` totals are NEVER summed on top of `bookkeepingEntries`.
  - Owner Allocation is reference-only and strictly excluded from payables, expenses, and settlement workflows.
- Manual Financial Entries (`bookkeepingEntries`):
  - Manual Income (`entryType: 'income'`) records non-invoice receipts cleanly without fabricating `invoices` or `paymentTransactions`.
  - Manual Expense (`Paid`) creates both `bookkeepingEntries` (`paidOnCreate: true`, settled) and `bookkeepingSettlements` atomically in one transaction; displayed once in Unified Ledger (`manual_expense`) and counted once in Expense Accrued & Cash Out.
  - Manual Expense (`Unpaid`) creates `bookkeepingEntries` (`unpaid`) only and enters Outstanding Payables with zero Cash Out until settled.
- Settlements (`bookkeepingSettlements`):
  - Append-only cash outflow ledger (`settlementType: 'expense_payment'`).
  - Operating Cash Out = `SUM(bookkeepingSettlements.amount)` filtered by `paidAt` in Asia/Jakarta.
- Inventory Boundary:
  - `inventoryItems` and `inventoryMovements` are completely isolated and never generate bookkeeping entries or financial expenses.

4. TRANSACTIONAL SETTLEMENT & REVERSAL ARCHITECTURE
============================================================
- `settleBookkeepingEntry()` (`services/bookkeeping-settlement-service.js`):
  - Runs inside a single Firestore `runTransaction` with all reads before writes.
  - Pre-generates deterministic `settlementId` (`operationId`) per dialog session so retries replay idempotently.
  - Validates active Owner, payable eligibility, `expectedVersion`, remaining balance (`> 0`), and rejects overpayment (`amount > remainingAmount`).
  - Atomically creates immutable `bookkeepingSettlements/{settlementId}` and updates payable `amountSettled`, `remainingAmount`, derived `settlementStatus` (`unpaid` | `partially_paid` | `paid`), `lastSettlementId`, `lastSettledAt`, `updatedAt`, `updatedByUid`, `version + 1`.
- `reverseBookkeepingEntry()` (`services/bookkeeping-entry-service.js`):
  - Owner-only correction strictly for manual entries with zero settlements (`sourceModule === 'bookkeeping' && sourceType === 'manual'`).
  - Requires audit reason (`>= 3` chars), preserves original entry (`settlementStatus: 'reversed'`), appends compensating `bk_rev_{entryId}`, and blocks reversing Commission accruals or already-settled manual expenses (no permanent deletes, no fabricated cash refunds).

5. FIRESTORE SECURITY RULES & BOUNDED QUERIES
============================================================
- Strictly Owner-only (`isActiveOwner()`) for all reads & writes across `bookkeepingEntries`, `bookkeepingSettlements`, and `bookkeepingSettings/default`. Staff, inactive accounts, and anonymous users are denied.

- Zero custom composite indexes required: all Firestore listeners use bounded single-field queries (`orderBy`, equality, or `settlementStatus in ['unpaid', 'partially_paid']`).
