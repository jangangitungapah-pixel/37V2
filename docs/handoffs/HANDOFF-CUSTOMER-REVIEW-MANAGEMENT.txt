1. OBJECTIVE & ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Replaced the previous third-party Google Maps Places review integration and manual
publicSiteSettings/site testimonial overrides with a first-party, booking-linked
Customer Review Management System operating strictly within the approved
Firebase Spark plan (Firebase Authentication + Cloud Firestore + Firebase Hosting).

End-to-end lifecycle:
1. Studio booking reaches sessionStatus == "completed" with actualCompletedAt,
   deliveryMode == "studio", valid customerId, and normalized phone number.
2. Active Staff or Owner opens the booking in Calendar -> BookingDetailDrawer
   and clicks "REQUEST CUSTOMER REVIEW" (or "Buat Ulang Undangan").
3. Client generates a high-entropy 64-hex random token (256 bits) in browser
   memory, hashes it with SHA-256, and atomically writes
   reviewInvitations/{tokenHash} + reviewInvitationIndexes/{bookingId}
   (revoking any prior active invitation during rotation).
4. ReviewInvitationShareDialog displays the transient /review/:token URL once
   with a copy-to-clipboard button and a manual WhatsApp Click-to-Chat link
   ("Open WhatsApp" -> https://wa.me/...). Staff manually chooses and sends in WhatsApp.
5. Customer visits public /review/:token (with <meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />),
   inspects non-PII session context, fills display name, 1-5 rating, written review,
   and mandatory consent checkbox (consentToPublish: true).
6. After optional Firebase App Check verification (ensureAppCheckTokenForPublicWrite)
   and low-cost honeypot check, client commits an atomic 2-write transaction:
   - creates customerReviews/{bookingId} with status == "pending"
   - updates reviewInvitations/{tokenHash} from "active" to "submitted"
7. Studio Owner opens Owner-only /admin/reviews ("Reviews" sidebar item, hidden
   for Staff and enforced by RequireOwner) to moderate pending/published/
   rejected/archived reviews without modifying customer review content/consent.
8. Publishing or unpublishing atomically updates publicReviewStats/summary
   (publishedCount, ratingSum, ratingDistribution).
9. Public Landing Page (CustomerReviewsSection directly after GallerySection and
   before PublicAvailabilityTeaserSection) subscribes only to published reviews
   (status == "published", limit 9) and publicReviewStats/summary.

5. FIRESTORE SCHEMA, SECURITY RULES & INDEXES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Collections added:
- reviewInvitations/{tokenHash}
- reviewInvitationIndexes/{bookingId}
- customerReviews/{bookingId}
- publicReviewStats/summary

Composite Index added to firestore.indexes.json:
- customerReviews: status ASC, publishedAt DESC (COLLECTION scope)

Factory Reset alignment:
- FACTORY_RESET_DELETION_MANIFEST updated from 18 to 20 targets:
  reviewInvitations (order 18) and reviewInvitationIndexes (order 19) are
  deleted right before bookings (order 20).
- customerReviews and publicReviewStats/summary have `allow delete: if false`
  in firestore.rules and are listed in FACTORY_RESET_PRESERVED_MANIFEST so
  published customer reviews survive Operational Factory Reset.

Note on booking normalization:
- calendar-booking-repository.js normalizeBookingRecord now preserves explicit
  data.deliveryMode even when serviceTypeId is unset, ensuring non-studio
  ('wfa' / 'remote') bookings are reliably detected in eligibility checks.
