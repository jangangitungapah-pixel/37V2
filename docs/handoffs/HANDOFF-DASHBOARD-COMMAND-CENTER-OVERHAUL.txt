2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Admin Dashboard (`/admin/dashboard`) has been transformed from a developer
module-shortcut placeholder into the production-grade 37 Music Studio Operations
Command Center.

The redesigned Command Center answers four operational questions in strict
priority order:
1. What is happening in the studio today? (Section A Header & Quick Actions +
   Section B Today's Overview 4-metric KPI row)
2. What sessions are coming next? (Section C Today's Studio Schedule grouped by
   Room A and Room B in chronological order)
3. What requires attention or action? (Section D Needs Attention queue ordered
   by business urgency)
4. How is the studio performing? (Section E Owner Financial Overview [Owner-only]
   + Section F Booking Trend Apache ECharts 6 visualizations)

5. QUERY & SUBSCRIPTION ARCHITECTURE (SPARK-ONLY, ZERO COMPOSITE INDEXES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`useDashboardData.js` reuses existing repository adapters with bounded,
single-field Firestore queries:
1. Today's Bookings (Owner & Staff):
   `bookingAdapter.subscribeBookingsInRange({ startDate: todayIso, endDate: todayIso, maxLimit: 300 })`
   Dedicated today query ensures Today's Overview and Today's Schedule are never
   starved by historical bookings in 30d/90d ranges.
2. Period Bookings for Booking Trend Chart (Owner & Staff):
   `bookingAdapter.subscribeBookingsInRange({ startDate: periodStart, endDate: periodEnd, maxLimit: 300 })`
3. Today's Invoices & Recent Invoices (Owner & Staff):
   `paymentAdapter.subscribeInvoicesInBookingDateRange({ startDate: todayIso, endDate: todayIso, maxLimit: 300 })`
   and `paymentAdapter.subscribeInvoices({ maxLimit: 100 })`, deduplicated by ID.
4. Inventory Items (Owner & Staff):
   `inventoryAdapter.subscribeInventoryItems({ maxLimit: 100 })`
5. Owner-Only Streams (gated by `if (!isOwner) return undefined;`):
   - `paymentAdapter.subscribePaymentTransactions({ maxLimit: 300 })`
   - `bookkeepingAdapter.subscribeBookkeepingEntries({ maxLimit: 300 })`
   - `bookkeepingAdapter.subscribeActivePayables({ maxLimit: 200 })`
   - `bookkeepingAdapter.subscribeBookkeepingSettlements({ maxLimit: 300 })`
   - `useCommissionReview({ enabled: isOwner, periodBounds: currentMonthBounds, maxLimit: 100 })`
All listeners return unsubscribe functions and are deterministically cleaned up
on unmount or period change.

6. FINANCIAL CALCULATION INTEGRITY RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Anti-Double-Counting of Customer Cash In:
   `Kas Masuk Hari Ini` and Period `Kas Masuk` are derived strictly from valid
   `paymentTransactions` (`SUM(payment) - SUM(reversal)`) plus paid non-reversed
   manual income entries (`isActiveManualIncomeEntry`). `invoice.amountPaid` is
   NEVER summed with `paymentTransactions`.
2. Actual Operating Cash Out vs Expense Accruals:
   `Kas Keluar` is derived strictly from `bookkeepingSettlements`
   (`settlementType === 'expense_payment'`). Unpaid `bookkeepingEntries`
   (`entryType === 'expense_accrual'`) are NEVER included in `Kas Keluar`; their
   open balance is reported under `Outstanding Payables`.
3. Accurate Net Cash Movement Terminology:
   `Perubahan Kas Bersih` (`Net Cash Movement`) is calculated as
   `Kas Masuk - Kas Keluar` and is explicitly subtitled
   `Net Cash Movement (Kas Masuk - Kas Keluar)`. It is never labeled as Profit
   or Laba Bersih.

7. SCHEDULE & NEEDS ATTENTION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Today's Studio Schedule groups today's (`Asia/Jakarta`) bookings into `Room A`
  (`room-a`) and `Room B` (`room-b`), excludes `cancelled` bookings from active
  session lists, and sorts chronologically by `startMinute ASC`.
- Needs Attention items are prioritized strictly by business urgency:
  1. Overdue Invoices (`isInvoiceOverdue(inv, todayIso)` on issued invoices)
  2. Today's Upcoming Sessions Past Start Time Awaiting Check-In
  3. Commission Review Awaiting Owner (Owner-only)
  4. Outstanding Payables Awaiting Settlement (Owner-only)
  5. Equipment Requiring Inspection or Maintenance (`hasMaintenanceAttention` or
     `damagedQuantity > 0`)
  6. Active Unpaid / Partial Invoices Not Yet Overdue

8. CHART ARCHITECTURE & DATA COVERAGE DISCLOSURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Uses installed Apache ECharts 6 (`echarts@6.1.0`) with `SVGRenderer`
  (`renderer: 'svg'`), `ResizeObserver`, `prefers-reduced-motion` support, and
  deterministic `chart.dispose()` cleanup on unmount.
- Zero fabricated, interpolated, or dummy data points.
- Explicit coverage banners render whenever any bounded real-time query reports
  truncation (`isTruncated` on `bookingTrend` or `hasPartialCoverage` across
  `paymentTransactions`, `bookkeepingSettlements`, `bookkeepingEntries`, and
  `activePayables`).
