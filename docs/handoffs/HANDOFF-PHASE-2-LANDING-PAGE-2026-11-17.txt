============================================================
1. PRODUCT BOUNDARY & USER CONFIRMATION
============================================================

- This public landing page at route "/" is built specifically for prospective
  37 Music Studio clients (bands, musicians, recording artists, and producers/
  creators) so they can explore the studio's services, inspect room setups,
  and submit a session inquiry.
- It is NOT a SaaS marketing page for other studio owners.
- The application is built exclusively for 37 Music Studio's internal
  management. The header and footer include a secondary "Login" button for
  future Owner/Operator access, which opens an accessible Base UI dialog
  explaining that internal staff login is coming in a future update.

6. Section 06 — Rooms ("Two rooms, zero guesswork") (`src/components/landing/RoomsSection.jsx`)
   - Two photo-led room cards (`Room A — Live Room` and `Room B — Control + Booth`)
     with acid-lime top-left stickers, draft capacity/setup lines, capability
     chips, and full-width black `Book this room` buttons that scroll to
     `#booking` and preselect that room.

7. Section 07 — Gallery ("Proof, not renders.") (`src/components/landing/GallerySection.jsx` & `GalleryLightboxDialog.jsx`)
   - Four-card studio detail grid with thick black borders, hard shadows, and
     bottom captions.
   - Lazy-loaded `@base-ui/react/dialog` lightbox viewer with keyboard-operable
     Prev/Next navigation.

8. Section 08 — Split Booking Process + Frontend-Only Booking Inquiry (`src/components/landing/BookingSection.jsx`)
   - Left panel (`#how-to-book`): Ink-black surface with acid-lime eyebrow,
     `Three taps to loud.` heading, 3 numbered process steps, and demo notice.
   - Right panel (`#booking`): Client-side validated inquiry form using
     `react-hook-form` + `zod` + `@hookform/resolvers/zod`.
   - Fields: Name / band name, WhatsApp / contact number, Requested service
     (tactile 3-button group), Room preference, Estimated duration, Preferred
     date, Preferred start time, and Notes.
   - On valid submit: displays the honest demo confirmation message
     ("Demo inquiry only — your request has not been sent. The booking
     integration will be added later.") with a summary and Edit/Reset controls.
     No data is sent to Firebase, an API, or localStorage.

9. Section 09 — Acid-Lime Testimonials + Location / Contact Card (`src/components/landing/TestimonialsAndContactSection.jsx`)
   - Acid-lime panel with two white bordered sample quote cards explicitly
     badged as `DEMO QUOTE` / `DEMO PREVIEW` awaiting owner-verified quotes.
   - White Location & Contact card (`#contact`) with honest pending notices for
     address, hours, and WhatsApp/phone, architectural preview image, and
     `Open in Maps` action that displays a pending-coordinates notice instead of
     opening a fabricated address.

10. Section 10 — Full-Width Signal-Orange Final CTA (`src/components/landing/TestimonialsAndContactSection.jsx`)
    - `DON’T REHEARSE IN A GARAGE.` banner with dark `Book a session` CTA and

11. Section 11 — Ink-Black Footer (`src/components/landing/SiteFooter.jsx`)
